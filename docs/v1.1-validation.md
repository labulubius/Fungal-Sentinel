# Fungal Sentinel v1.1 validation

## Automated checks

Run:

```bash
./gradlew testDebugUnitTest lintDebug assembleRelease
```

The unit suite checks B/R wavelength fitting with G hold-out validation, ascending wavelength ordering for a negative pixel/nm slope, positive trapezoidal integration, SPD CSV parsing, and concentration regression.

## Device acceptance checklist

Use a Camera2 device exposing `MANUAL_SENSOR` and `RAW`.

1. Install v1.1 over v1.0 with the same signing key and confirm the app starts.
2. Confirm preview, ISO, exposure, focus, white balance, noise reduction, edge and hot-pixel controls still work.
3. Capture a normal DNG and inspect it with `exiftool`.
4. Open **Analyze** and complete Step 1 with the R/G/B positioning source.
   - Verify all three peaks are visible.
   - A G error up to 2.5 nm is PASS, up to 10 nm is WARNING, and above 10 nm is rejected.
5. Import a real two-column SPD CSV and complete Step 2.
   - Verify three finite response curves are shown over 420–680 nm.
6. Select a fluorophore and complete Step 3.
   - Verify area and peak are non-negative and repeat analysis is stable.
7. Capture at least three standards and calculate Step 4.
   - Verify equation, R², predicted concentration, and out-of-range warning.
8. Confirm DNG files appear under `Pictures/FungalSentinel` with `positioning_`, `spd_`, `sample_`, or `standard_` prefixes.

## Deliberate failure checks

- Use an overexposed frame: analysis must reject it while preserving the DNG.
- Change ISO, exposure, focus, camera/CFA, or RAW size after Step 1: subsequent analysis must reject the frame.
- Skip Step 1 or true-SPD import: dependent capture buttons remain disabled.
- Import malformed SPD data: the app reports an error and does not crash.
- Use identical standard concentrations: regression is rejected.
- Predict outside the standards' range: an extrapolation warning is shown.

## Scientific limitation

The app implements the FSSA numerical workflow but does not establish biological detection thresholds. Concentration claims still require validated blanks, at least 3–5 standards, replicates, reference measurements, and device-specific calibration.
